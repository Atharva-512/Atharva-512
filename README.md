## About Me

I design systems that turn messy, real-world data into something a business can actually query, trust, and act on. My focus is the layer between raw ingestion and the dashboard — building ELT pipelines with clean separation of concerns (Bronze → Silver → Gold), warehouses modeled around how the business actually asks questions (star schemas, not flat exports), and reporting layers that hold up under real usage.

I care about pipelines that are **idempotent, incremental, and observable** over ones that just "work once." Most of what I build starts as a batch pipeline problem, gets modeled as a dimensional warehouse, and ends as a Power BI or Tableau layer someone in the business actually opens every morning.

Currently sharpening the cloud side of this — Azure Data Factory, Databricks, and Synapse — while keeping the fundamentals (SQL modeling, data quality, dashboard design) equally sharp.
